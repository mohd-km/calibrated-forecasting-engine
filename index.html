Methodology, Formulas & Forward Ledger

Pre-Registered Rule & Live k Sweep

The pre-registered rule applies logistic extremization p_model = sigmoid(k · logit(p_market)) with default k = 1.15 (clamped to [1%, 99%]). Values k < 1 shrink crowd prices toward 50%; values k > 1 push probabilities toward the tails.

At k = 1.15 and k = 1.00, scores match the exact market-level build-time backtest; intermediate k values recompute live from the committed domain decile distribution preserving within-bin variance.

Dataset, Horizon & Honest Reporting

Backtest metrics are computed at build time from real resolved Manifold binary markets sampled exactly 7 days before close/resolution (/v0/bets). When a mechanical rule trails the crowd, the negative Brier skill is reported directly.

    Why Proper Scoring Rules Penalize Overconfidence

    Both Brier Score (p − y)² and Log-Loss −[y ln(p) + (1 − y) ln(1 − p)] are strictly proper: your expected loss is uniquely minimized when your reported probability equals the true event probability p*. Log-loss diverges toward infinity as a confident forecast misses, with excess expected loss equal to the Kullback–Leibler divergence D_KL(p* || q).
